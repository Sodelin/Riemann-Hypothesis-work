### 12.6 What was not treated as proof

- finite verification of zeros or inequalities;
- neural or latent-space novelty by itself;
- numerical nonnegativity without tail control;
- source-reported certificates not independently rerun;
- any statement whose missing lemma is equivalent in strength to RH;
- agreement among agents sharing model lineage;
- “no source found” as a proof of novelty;
- analytic continuation as preservation of termwise positivity.

### 12.7 External validation gate

Before a public mathematical claim, the minimum package is:

1. a standalone proof in which every coefficient bound and tail estimate is explicit;
2. independent reconstruction by a human specialist in entire functions;
3. a second human review focused on prior art;
4. MathSciNet and zbMATH searches, including related work by Muranaka, Csordas, Cardon, Newman, Dimitrov, Rusev, and Vishnyakova;
5. symbolic spot checks for several \(N\), clearly labeled quality assurance rather than proof;
6. disclosure of the multi-agent LLM derivation, model/version information if available, and the exact human checks performed.
